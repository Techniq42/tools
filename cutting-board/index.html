Cutting Board

A small, honest video editor that runs entirely in your browser. Trim a clip, join a few together, burn in a caption, pull the audio, or switch between MP4 and WebM. Nothing is uploaded — the video never leaves your device.

●Private by design. Every edit happens on your own machine with ffmpeg.wasm. No account, no upload, no server, $0.
●Built for short clips. Great for trims, titles, and stitching a few phone clips. Very large or 4K files may be slow or run out of memory — this is not a 4K feature NLE.
●First run downloads ~30 MB of editor engine (once, then cached by your browser). Needs a connection the first time; works on phones and laptops.
Editor engine will load the first time you run an edit.

Trim a clip

Set an in-point and an out-point, then export just that stretch. Use the buttons to grab the time from the source player as you scrub.

MP4 / WebM re-encode for frame-accurate cuts. Keep original is much faster but cuts land on the nearest keyframe, so the start can be slightly off.

Join clips end to end

Add two or more clips above (drop or tap the zone again to add more). They'll be stitched in the order listed — drag isn't needed, remove and re-add to reorder. Clips are normalised to 1280×720, 30 fps so mismatched sizes still line up, so this one re-encodes everything and is the slowest operation.

Burn in a caption or title

Text is rendered permanently onto the picture. Good for a title card line or a lower-third. Keep it short.

Extract the audio

Pull the soundtrack out of a clip as a standalone audio file — handy for podcasts, transcripts, or a voice reference.

Convert the format

Switch a clip between MP4 (H.264 / AAC — plays almost everywhere) and WebM (VP9 / Opus — open and web-friendly).

Cutting Board — a Fellowship of Living Systems practitioner tool, part of the free tool shelf. The pattern: we needed it, we aimed the AI at it, and we gave it away.

    
Privacy: everything runs in your browser — your video is never uploaded and never leaves your device. Limits: built for short clips, trims and titles; very large or 4K files may be slow or run out of memory (browsers cap this kind of work around 2–4 GB).

    
Licenses & source. This tool is a single static page — its full source is this page (view source), released under the Apache License 2.0; its wording and design under CC BY 4.0. The editing is done by ffmpeg.wasm, a WebAssembly build of FFmpeg, loaded at runtime from a public CDN (unpkg) — not redistributed from this server. FFmpeg and ffmpeg.wasm are free software under the LGPL/GPL; their complete corresponding source is published and downloadable: FFmpeg at github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg and the WebAssembly build at github.com/ffmpegwasm/ffmpeg.wasm. The caption typeface is Inter (SIL Open Font License). Fork it, clone it, carry the byline — the tool was never the asset.
