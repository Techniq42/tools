Follow Teleprompter

20-second start

  1. ✎ Script — paste your own words (or keep the sample).
  2. ● Camera — optional, turns on your front camera so you can record.
  3. 🎤 Follow my voice — then just start reading out loud. The text scrolls to keep up with you.

No voice-follow? Tap ▶ Scroll for steady auto-scroll, or drag the text with your finger to move it yourself.

Reading in another language? Pick it in the voice lang menu (bottom bar) so voice-follow listens correctly. Dragging and auto-scroll work in any language, with or without the internet.

Works offline

Manual scroll and recording always work, even with no internet — that is the floor you can count on. Follow-my-voice is the extra magic for when you are online: it uses your browser's speech recognition, which needs a connection and works best in Chrome or Edge (it is not available in some browsers, like iPhone Safari).

Your privacy

When you turn on Follow my voice, your browser sends audio to its speech-recognition service to match your words — that is how voice-follow works, and it needs the internet. Your recording stays on your device: the video is saved straight to your downloads and is never uploaded by this tool.
